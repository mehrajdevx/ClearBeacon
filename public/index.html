    CALLER SAFETY

    Unexpected caller? Verify safely

    ClearBeacon does not identify or rate phone numbers. Caller ID can be spoofed.

    • Do not share codes, passwords, payment details, or remote access.
    • Hang up and call the organization back using a number from its official app, card, or website you type yourself.
    • Do not rely on a search ad or the caller's callback number to verify them.
